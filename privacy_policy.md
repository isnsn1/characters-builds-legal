# Privacy Policy for Characters Builds

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026
**Developer:** qi1

## What data we collect
- Discord user ID and username of players
- Game points and scores
- Server name and server ID

We do **not** collect real names, emails, or private messages.

## Message content
The bot reads messages in channels where it is enabled, only to detect commands and check game answers. Message content is processed in real time and is **not stored or logged**. In channels where the bot is not enabled, messages are ignored.

## How we use your data
Data is used solely for leaderboards (top players and top servers) and game functionality. We do not sell, share, or disclose data to any third party.

## Data security
Data is stored securely and is accessible only by the developer.

## Data removal
You may request deletion of your data or your server's data at any time by contacting the developer.

By using Characters Builds, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
